count is the Nth matching op after arming, not an absolute ordinal — counters start at zero when the rule is installed. times is how often it may fire before retiring. conn takes any, next, or an id. after op takes an op type, connect, or one of this emulator's op classes: .

advance_clock is immediate — it applies the moment you post it and takes no trigger, so after op, count, times and conn are ignored. This is how a lesson ages the cache without sleeping. 

a rule naming an op the emulator cannot produce is rejected here, not silently dead.
